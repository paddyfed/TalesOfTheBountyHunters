July was a very hot and dry month and the totals reflect that fact. It hasn't beaten May 2025 in terms of energy consumed and exported but it did turn out to be the second best month for those totals. Exported total was higher than usual due to the hot water reaching the maximum temperature more often. We did have the cars plugged in though which meant the imported totals between 2am and 4am were down slightly.

This month the generated total was 494kWh with 322kWh exported. So total generation was 816kWh. So still a bit off May 2025 with it's 945kWh generation. Import total was 320kWh.
