Another one I'd not seen before. I don't think this got the best of reviews at the time but I also hadn't been too interested in X-Men from the previous ones. This one was confusing. I wasn't really sure what was going on with the plot at times and the old man being in the metal suit at the end was a bit obvious. The first 20 minutes I did enjoy though. Best bit was the post credits scene though which I presume is leading into Days of Future Past.
