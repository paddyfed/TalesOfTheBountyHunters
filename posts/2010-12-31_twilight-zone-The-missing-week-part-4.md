> Here is the final set of posts that I found in a diary. This brigs us up to New Year's Eve in 2010. This was added in June 2026.

Monday: I was in work for 9 and Paula stayed at home, I didn't get to the shop. I headed out with Paula, we got a lift in with Sean. Met John and Emily in the Pig. We went to McKennas and met with Brian, Marie and Lorraine. Gavin, Sorcha and Seamie were in also. We went back to the Pig for a few and we were going to go home but we went to Mexx instead. It was a very good night and I got home around 4 in the morning.

Tuesday: I was in work at 10 and was very tired. Tried to organise football but we could only get 5. Eamonn and Patricia were over and stayed for dinner. I was still very tired when heading to bed. I talked to Paula for a while. I think she is getting worried about going back to work.

Wednesday: I was working from 12-4 and panaged to get to the shop. Paula met up with Edel in Carrick and went out to dinner with the girls that evening. Becky and Emma also went out. I started going through the C# book.

Thursday: I was working until 4 again. Paula came into town and went for a scone and got things for dinner. I bought a new United top. We were going to go to the conema but it was far too foggy. Charlotte was home for a night so we went to visit her. After dinner we got back to Paula's house and went to bed. Paula sat with me for a while.

Friday: (New Year's Eve) I was in work most of the day until 3 and Paula picked me up. Gerard and Elaine went to Caroline's. Sean and Margaret went to Dympna's. Paula made a cake during the day and we had chicken in sweet pepper sauce with spring rolls for starter. It was lovely. We watched Jools Holland for the countdown and hopefully 2011 will be a better year for everybody.

Paddy.
